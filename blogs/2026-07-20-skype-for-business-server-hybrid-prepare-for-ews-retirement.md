The Exchange team recently announced a phased retirement of Exchange Web Services (EWS) in Exchange Online, with full retirement on April 1, 2027 . See Exchange Online EWS, Your Time is Almost Up for the full background. This change affects hybrid Skype for Business Server deployments, which use EWS today to communicate with Exchange Online.
