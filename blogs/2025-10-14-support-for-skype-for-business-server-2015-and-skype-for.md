Today , October 14, 2025, Skype for Business Server 2015 , Skype for Business Server 2019 , Skype for Business 2016 (client), and Skype for Business 2019 (client) reach end of support. It’s therefore critical to upgrade now to remain supported and secure. Microsoft will no longer provide technical support, meaning some problems may occur with these products.
