Over the last several weeks we have received several questions about possible extension of the Skype for Business Server 2015/2019 ESU program past October 2026. After all, in our original Skype for Business 2015 / 2019 Extended Security Update program announcement we said that there would be no extensions, but then we ended up creating a “Period 2” Skype for Business ESU program , that is scheduled to conclude at the end of October 2026. We are just about at the mid-point of Period 2 Exchange ESU now.
